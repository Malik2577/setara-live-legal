Child Safety Standards & Protection Policy

Zero tolerance: Setara Live prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, and any behavior that endangers children.

1. Scope and age requirements

These standards apply to all users, profiles, live voice rooms, messages, images, gifts, and other content or interactions on Setara Live. Setara Live is intended for users aged 16 and older. Being above the minimum age does not reduce protections for anyone under 18.

2. Prohibited behavior and content

  • Creating, sharing, requesting, promoting, or distributing CSAM, including links to such material.
  • Grooming, sexual exploitation, coercion, sextortion, trafficking, or soliciting minors for sexual activity.
  • Sexualized images or descriptions of children, or attempts to arrange exploitative contact with minors.
  • Threats, harassment, or efforts to evade child safety enforcement.

3. Reporting safety concerns

Users and members of the public can report suspected child exploitation, abuse, grooming, or other child safety concerns directly to our safety contact at setaravoicelive@gmail.com. Include relevant user or room identifiers, dates, and a brief description. Do not email or redistribute illegal child sexual abuse material.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or relevant child protection authorities. Reports can also be made to the appropriate national child protection reporting organization.

4. Review and enforcement

Setara Live's safety policy is to review child safety reports, restrict access to potentially harmful content where appropriate, and take action against accounts involved in violations, including suspension or permanent removal. We aim to preserve relevant information when legally required and cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities.

5. Legal compliance and escalation

Setara Live is committed to complying with applicable child protection and online safety laws, and to reporting confirmed child sexual exploitation or abuse to the relevant authorities when legally required. We do not permit users to use the service to facilitate harm to children.

6. Child safety contact

For questions, urgent reports, or communication about these standards, contact: setaravoicelive@gmail.com.

7. Policy updates

We may revise these standards as our safety processes, features, or legal obligations change. The current version will be published on this page.
