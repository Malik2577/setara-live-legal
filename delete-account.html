Delete Your Setara Live Account

Setara Live allows users to request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the Setara Live app.
  2. Go to Me > Settings > Delete Account.
  3. Enter a reason (optional).
  4. Tap Submit request.

You can also request account deletion by emailing setaravoicelive@gmail.com. Include your registered email address and write "Account Deletion Request" in the subject.

What data is deleted?

After verification and processing, we delete your account, profile information, and associated personal data, including applicable user-generated content.

Data retention

Some information may be retained where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, or financial recordkeeping. Such data is deleted when the applicable retention period ends.

Processing

Your account may remain active while your deletion request is being reviewed and processed.
